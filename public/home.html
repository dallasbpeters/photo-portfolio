Photo Portfolio

An online gallery and photo management tool. Publish your photography to the web, organize it into categories, and manage your work from a private admin area.

What the app does

Photo Portfolio turns a folder of photographs into a polished, public-facing gallery. Visitors can browse your work by category and view each image in detail. The owner signs in to a private admin area to add, organize and publish photos — without editing a line of code.

Features

  • Publish a personal photography gallery with your own branding.
  • Upload, organize and manage a library of photographs.
  • Group photos into categories and control what is public.
  • Edit images with a built-in grading tool (tone, color, crop and rotate).
  • A private admin area to keep your work in order.

Who it's for

Photographers and creators who want their work online with minimal fuss. The admin area is accessed securely — you can sign in with Google rather than managing a separate password. Your published gallery is at dallaspeters.com.
